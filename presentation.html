The Recruitment Paradox

Standard search systems rely solely on lexical keyword matches (missing context/intent) or raw semantic embeddings (which dilution scores for niche technical skills). This project builds an end-to-end multi-stage architecture that fuses dense semantic matching, sparse exact keyword matching, and LTR machine learning features.

  • Stage 1: Hybrid sparse & dense indexing retrieves the most matching profiles.
  • Stage 2: Multi-query Learning-to-Rank LTR scores structural properties.
  • Stage 3: Cross-Encoder deep Transformer scoring matches pure semantics.
  • Stage 4: MMR diversity filters candidates to prevent redundant slates.

Offline Knowledge Distillation & Feature Engineering

⚙️

Tabular Engineering

Normalization parsed from JSONL to SQLite. We extract 46 structural candidate features including:

  • Career continuity & stability: Job change frequency, career gaps, promotion velocity.
  • Education: Academic tiering (Tier 1 vs. rest), GPA strength.
  • Logistics: Notice period, salary expectations.
🧠

Knowledge Distillation

LTR requires high-quality relevance scores which manual recruitment databases lack. We distill relevance labels using an offline teacher:

  • Neural Teacher: High-capacity transformer model (ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2) scores full profiles.
  • Quantile Binning: Continuous scores binned into discrete classes [0, 1, 2, 3] representing mismatch to outstanding profiles.
🛡️

Adversarial Defense

Modern recruitment databases face prompt-injections, spam profiles, and resume keyword-stuffing.

  • Honeypot flags: Detection algorithms look for hidden prompt-stuffing flags.
  • Adversarial Penalty: Detected honeypots are forced to score 0 relevance in distillation to train the LTR student models to penalize adversarial behavior.

The Real-Time Ranking & Explainability Engine

🔮

Dual-Objective Scoring & Blending

The ranking engine uses predictions from the XGBoost models trained on the distilled dataset:

  • Pairwise + NDCG Ensemble: Combines a pairwise ranker (minimizes relative swaps) and listwise ranker (NDCG optimizer).
  • Deep Semantic Fusion: The top LTR candidates are scored by the Cross-Encoder model. The final score is a 50% structural features (LTR) + 50% semantic relevance (Cross-Encoder) blend.
🎭

Diversity & Explainable Decisions

Once scored, candidates are filtered using post-processing engines to ensure they are high-quality, diverse, and transparent:

  • MMR Diversity (λ=0.85): Maximizes candidate relevance while penalizing similarity to already selected profiles. Prevents presenting 100 identical candidates.
  • Structured Explainability: Formulates candidate justifications in natural language: listing skills matched, skills missing, risk indicators (long notice, hop index), and confidence.

Results & Performance

Results

Technologies Used

What technologies, frameworks, and tools were used and why were they selected for this solution?

  • FAISS (Facebook AI Similarity Search): Selected for high-speed dense semantic similarity retrieval over section embeddings.
  • BM25 (Rank-BM25): Selected to ensure exact keyword match for specific developer skills, preventing dilution.
  • XGBoost (XGBRanker): Fast and powerful student LTR ranker that fits non-linear combinations of 46 features.
  • SentenceTransformers: BGE-base-en-v1.5 and ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2 select dense semantics and CrossEncoder matching.
  • SQLite (Relational Storage): Serverless, simple SQL db for fast candidates metadata & features retrieval.
  • Streamlit (Recruiter Portal): Provides a lightweight, python-native UI wrapper for prompt query exploration.
